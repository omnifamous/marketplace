# AI visibility research

A measured study, not an opinion piece. 7,949 prompts, 5 engines, 3 waves,
39,572 captured answers. Every figure in it reproduces from a published
evidence register.

## Why read it before answering

Questions about what AI engines cite are usually answered from intuition, and
the intuitions are stale: the cited web turned over substantially in four
months, and two engines now expose far fewer sources than they used to. Numbers
recalled from training data will be confidently wrong.
